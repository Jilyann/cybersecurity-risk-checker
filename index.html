Security Recommendations

Complete the assessment to receive recommendations based on your general risk level.

Password and Account Security

  • Use strong and unique passwords.
  • Avoid sharing passwords with other people.
  • Use a trusted password manager when appropriate.

Multi-Factor Authentication

  • Enable MFA when available.
  • Review verification requests carefully.
  • Never approve an unexpected login request.

Phishing Awareness

  • Check the sender before responding.
  • Be cautious of urgent or unusual requests.
  • Verify suspicious links before opening them.
