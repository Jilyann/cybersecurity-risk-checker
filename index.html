Welcome

This browser-based application helps college students review their online security habits and cybersecurity awareness.

The assessment focuses on password and account security, multi-factor authentication, and phishing awareness.

Security Recommendations

After completing the assessment, users may receive a general risk level and practical recommendations based on their answers.

Possible Recommendations

  • Use strong and unique passwords.
  • Enable multi-factor authentication when available.
  • Check suspicious links before opening them.
  • Be cautious when sharing personal information online.

Quick Security Reminder

Never share real passwords or sensitive account information while using cybersecurity awareness tools.

Remember:

  • Think before clicking links.
  • Protect your accounts.
  • Verify suspicious messages.
